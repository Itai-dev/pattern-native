# Team loop · 18 Oct 2026

A two-hour session run as rounds: five roles (founder, go-to-market,
UX/UI, copy, chronic-pain research) each did a focused read of the app
and the docs, then met, decided one small over-the-air change, and built
it with `npm run verify` green. Nothing here is published until the
owner merges and runs the EAS update — the session's EAS token was
invalid.

Standing rule from the founder for the whole session: **Today's layout,
order, the ramp and the orb are off limits.** Today was rebuilt five
times between 28 and 30 Sep; a sixth redesign is the drift itself, and a
week-4 retention number means nothing if the screen changes under it.

## Round 1 · 21:07–21:14 UTC

**What the roles found**

- *Founder / research (independently):* the activity card learns only
  from mornings — a session's outcome is the first check-in between
  05:00 and noon on its day against the next (`health/capacity.ts`) —
  but the only reminder Today offers turns on the 20:00 slot. A tester
  who says yes is steered to check-ins the card cannot read, and can sit
  on "still learning" into week four. This is the riskiest Phase 2
  assumption, and it is self-inflicted.
- *Design:* a fresh check-in starts live at 5, the slider seeded its last
  step at 5, so a tap AT 5 changed nothing — no haptic, Done stayed dim.
  VoiceOver could only reach 5 by swiping away and back. The core action
  of the app failed for the one value it starts on.
- *Copy:* the evening lock-screen reminder said "A missed day is just a
  missed day" — mentioning the missed day to exactly the person who
  missed it, breaking Today's promise that a reminder never does. The
  sleep prompt opened "Good morning." to someone who may have woken in
  pain.
- *GTM:* week-4 retention is not countable — `analytics.ts` has no
  install id and mints a session per cold start. Health is offered last
  although its 90-day backfill would give a watch wearer the card's
  answer on day one. Build 48 expires around 14 Dec, mid-Phase 2.

**Decided**

1. A tap at five counts; VoiceOver double-tap chooses (`Slider.tsx`,
   `chosen` prop; `CheckinScreen.tsx` passes `moved`).
2. Beside an activity card, the one-tap offer is a morning reminder
   (`enableMorningReminder`), and says why. Without one, evening stays.
   The button does not name an hour, because the planner moves the
   morning slot to half an hour after the usual wake.
3. The two lock-screen strings rewritten.
4. `tools/test-checkin-tap.js` guards all three.

**Parked, with reasons:** Health-first ordering (a day-one permission ask
to privacy-minded sceptics needs its own argument); week-4 counting
(changes the closed analytics list — needs GROWTH/POSITIONING first);
44pt targets (next round); the outcome-vocabulary pass (next round).

## Round 2 · 21:14–21:22 UTC

**Briefs:** copy unifies the outcome's names; design makes touch targets
44pt without moving anything; research tests the flare idea harder; GTM
takes the recruiting surface; the founder reviews round 1 as the person
who will publish it.

**Decided and built**

1. **One name for the outcome.** "Went fine" and "a harder next
   morning", across `recovery.ts` and `health/capacity.ts`. Gone: "well
   tolerated", "your body handled", "is costing you the next morning",
   "stable morning", "baseline", "Try…", "keep it easy", "Stay at". The
   researcher's caveat stands on the record: "went fine" means next
   morning less than `CAPACITY_HARDER_POINTS` above this one, so it holds
   only because the card's own note defines it. One advice change rode
   along on purpose: an early single harder morning now offers "the same
   length again, rather than longer" instead of "keep today lighter",
   matching the hold state and the threshold's "one harder morning is a
   day".
2. **A flare day does not get "longer".** When a flare is logged for
   today, the step-up becomes the same length at the person's own level,
   with one line: "You logged a flare today, so this isn't suggesting
   longer. Your own flare plan, or your clinician's, comes first."
   Rejected: a caution under "a little longer" (two messages, the action
   wins); fewer minutes (a flare plan written by the app); hiding the
   suggestion (reads as "don't move"). Gated on the person's report only,
   never a pain value. Nothing reaches the engine.
3. **44pt without moving a pixel.** Offer buttons, the "Add information"
   row, goal chips and the Day/Calendar switch get hitSlop; four targets
   round 1 flagged were already 44 inside their frames. VoiceOver reaches
   "Add information" through a named action on Today's day card.
4. **Founder review fixes.** VoiceOver double-tap gives the haptic; the
   squares picker takes the same double-tap; `reminder_enabled` carries
   which slot (`m`/`e`), so the morning fix can be seen to land; the
   sleep prompt says "right now" (it can fire at 13:00).
5. **Docs told the truth again.** ROADMAP: the experiment left the app on
   30 Sep. POSITIONING: `ActivityIntention.tsx` was deleted on 28 Sep.
   GROWTH §2 and `analytics.ts`: week-4 retention is NOT computable —
   no install id, a session per cold start.
6. **Landing page** (no publish needed): the invite email opens with
   three optional screening questions; the mock shows the one-button
   check-in; the backup claim says a reinstall is not covered by the
   phone's backup, a saved copy is; "no server" became "no Pattern
   server", since Aptabase is named just above it.

**Known and left alone this round**

- Testers who already took the evening offer keep it and never see the
  morning one. That respects the Today freeze; the morning reminder is
  in Profile for them.
- Onboarding's first screen still pitches the diary-for-the-doctor
  ("Make pain feel less random"). GTM wants "Understand your pain. Keep
  living." to match the landing. Held for round 3 with the copywriter.
- After noon, the activity card's "Check in" button still asks for a
  check-in that can no longer answer the morning question
  (`dailyGuidance` has no clock). Round 3.
- Build 48 expires around 14 Dec, inside Phase 2. Needs `eas build`
  from the owner's computer before then.
