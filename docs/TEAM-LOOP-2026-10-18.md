# Team loop · 18 Oct 2026

A two-hour session run as rounds: five roles (founder, go-to-market,
UX/UI, copy, chronic-pain research) each did a focused read of the app
and the docs, then met, decided one small over-the-air change, and built
it with `npm run verify` green. Nothing here is published until the
owner merges and runs the EAS update — the session's EAS token was
invalid.

Standing rule from the founder for the whole session: **Today's layout,
order, the ramp and the orb are off limits.** Today was rebuilt five
times between 28 and 30 Sep; a sixth redesign is the drift itself, and a
week-4 retention number means nothing if the screen changes under it.

## Round 1 · 21:07–21:35 UTC

**What the roles found**

- *Founder / research (independently):* the activity card learns only
  from mornings — a session's outcome is the first check-in between
  05:00 and noon on its day against the next (`health/capacity.ts`) —
  but the only reminder Today offers turns on the 20:00 slot. A tester
  who says yes is steered to check-ins the card cannot read, and can sit
  on "still learning" into week four. This is the riskiest Phase 2
  assumption, and it is self-inflicted.
- *Design:* a fresh check-in starts live at 5, the slider seeded its last
  step at 5, so a tap AT 5 changed nothing — no haptic, Done stayed dim.
  VoiceOver could only reach 5 by swiping away and back. The core action
  of the app failed for the one value it starts on.
- *Copy:* the evening lock-screen reminder said "A missed day is just a
  missed day" — mentioning the missed day to exactly the person who
  missed it, breaking Today's promise that a reminder never does. The
  sleep prompt opened "Good morning." to someone who may have woken in
  pain.
- *GTM:* week-4 retention is not countable — `analytics.ts` has no
  install id and mints a session per cold start. Health is offered last
  although its 90-day backfill would give a watch wearer the card's
  answer on day one. Build 48 expires around 14 Dec, mid-Phase 2.

**Decided**

1. A tap at five counts; VoiceOver double-tap chooses (`Slider.tsx`,
   `chosen` prop; `CheckinScreen.tsx` passes `moved`).
2. Beside an activity card, the one-tap offer is a morning reminder
   (`enableMorningReminder`), and says why. Without one, evening stays.
   The button does not name an hour, because the planner moves the
   morning slot to half an hour after the usual wake.
3. The two lock-screen strings rewritten.
4. `tools/test-checkin-tap.js` guards all three.

**Parked, with reasons:** Health-first ordering (a day-one permission ask
to privacy-minded sceptics needs its own argument); week-4 counting
(changes the closed analytics list — needs GROWTH/POSITIONING first);
44pt targets (next round); the outcome-vocabulary pass (next round).
