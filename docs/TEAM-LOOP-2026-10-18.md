# Team loop · 18 Oct 2026

A two-hour session run as rounds: five roles (founder, go-to-market,
UX/UI, copy, chronic-pain research) each did a focused read of the app
and the docs, then met, decided one small over-the-air change, and built
it with `npm run verify` green. Nothing here is published until the
owner merges and runs the EAS update — the session's EAS token was
invalid.

Standing rule from the founder for the whole session: **Today's layout,
order, the ramp and the orb are off limits.** Today was rebuilt five
times between 28 and 30 Sep; a sixth redesign is the drift itself, and a
week-4 retention number means nothing if the screen changes under it.

## Round 1 · 21:07–21:14 UTC

**What the roles found**

- *Founder / research (independently):* the activity card learns only
  from mornings — a session's outcome is the first check-in between
  05:00 and noon on its day against the next (`health/capacity.ts`) —
  but the only reminder Today offers turns on the 20:00 slot. A tester
  who says yes is steered to check-ins the card cannot read, and can sit
  on "still learning" into week four. This is the riskiest Phase 2
  assumption, and it is self-inflicted.
- *Design:* a fresh check-in starts live at 5, the slider seeded its last
  step at 5, so a tap AT 5 changed nothing — no haptic, Done stayed dim.
  VoiceOver could only reach 5 by swiping away and back. The core action
  of the app failed for the one value it starts on.
- *Copy:* the evening lock-screen reminder said "A missed day is just a
  missed day" — mentioning the missed day to exactly the person who
  missed it, breaking Today's promise that a reminder never does. The
  sleep prompt opened "Good morning." to someone who may have woken in
  pain.
- *GTM:* week-4 retention is not countable — `analytics.ts` has no
  install id and mints a session per cold start. Health is offered last
  although its 90-day backfill would give a watch wearer the card's
  answer on day one. Build 48 expires around 14 Dec, mid-Phase 2.

**Decided**

1. A tap at five counts; VoiceOver double-tap chooses (`Slider.tsx`,
   `chosen` prop; `CheckinScreen.tsx` passes `moved`).
2. Beside an activity card, the one-tap offer is a morning reminder
   (`enableMorningReminder`), and says why. Without one, evening stays.
   The button does not name an hour, because the planner moves the
   morning slot to half an hour after the usual wake.
3. The two lock-screen strings rewritten.
4. `tools/test-checkin-tap.js` guards all three.

**Parked, with reasons:** Health-first ordering (a day-one permission ask
to privacy-minded sceptics needs its own argument); week-4 counting
(changes the closed analytics list — needs GROWTH/POSITIONING first);
44pt targets (next round); the outcome-vocabulary pass (next round).

## Round 2 · 21:14–21:22 UTC

**Briefs:** copy unifies the outcome's names; design makes touch targets
44pt without moving anything; research tests the flare idea harder; GTM
takes the recruiting surface; the founder reviews round 1 as the person
who will publish it.

**Decided and built**

1. **One name for the outcome.** "Went fine" and "a harder next
   morning", across `recovery.ts` and `health/capacity.ts`. Gone: "well
   tolerated", "your body handled", "is costing you the next morning",
   "stable morning", "baseline", "Try…", "keep it easy", "Stay at". The
   researcher's caveat stands on the record: "went fine" means next
   morning less than `CAPACITY_HARDER_POINTS` above this one, so it holds
   only because the card's own note defines it. One advice change rode
   along on purpose: an early single harder morning now offers "the same
   length again, rather than longer" instead of "keep today lighter",
   matching the hold state and the threshold's "one harder morning is a
   day".
2. **A flare day does not get "longer".** When a flare is logged for
   today, the step-up becomes the same length at the person's own level,
   with one line: "You logged a flare today, so this isn't suggesting
   longer. Your own flare plan, or your clinician's, comes first."
   Rejected: a caution under "a little longer" (two messages, the action
   wins); fewer minutes (a flare plan written by the app); hiding the
   suggestion (reads as "don't move"). Gated on the person's report only,
   never a pain value. Nothing reaches the engine.
3. **44pt without moving a pixel.** Offer buttons, the "Add information"
   row, goal chips and the Day/Calendar switch get hitSlop; four targets
   round 1 flagged were already 44 inside their frames. VoiceOver reaches
   "Add information" through a named action on Today's day card.
4. **Founder review fixes.** VoiceOver double-tap gives the haptic; the
   squares picker takes the same double-tap; `reminder_enabled` carries
   which slot (`m`/`e`), so the morning fix can be seen to land; the
   sleep prompt says "right now" (it can fire at 13:00).
5. **Docs told the truth again.** ROADMAP: the experiment left the app on
   30 Sep. POSITIONING: `ActivityIntention.tsx` was deleted on 28 Sep.
   GROWTH §2 and `analytics.ts`: week-4 retention is NOT computable —
   no install id, a session per cold start.
6. **Landing page** (no publish needed): the invite email opens with
   three optional screening questions; the mock shows the one-button
   check-in; the backup claim says a reinstall is not covered by the
   phone's backup, a saved copy is; "no server" became "no Pattern
   server", since Aptabase is named just above it.

**Drafts for the owner (not sent anywhere)**

Recruiting post, physio noticeboard or running-club chat — the clinic
has to agree before it goes up:

> Looking for about 10 people to try an iPhone app I'm building, called
> Pattern. It's for people with recurring pain — back, knee, tendons,
> joints, inflammatory — who still train, or are working back to it. One

> reads your own sessions and next mornings and suggests the next small

TestFlight "What to Test":

> this build expires — save a copy in Profile: your iPhone's backup will

**Known and left alone this round**

  in Profile for them.
- Onboarding's first screen still pitches the diary-for-the-doctor

  living." to match the landing. Held for round 3 with the copywriter.
- After noon, the activity card's "Check in" button still asks for a
  check-in that can no longer answer the morning question

## Round 3 · 21:22–21:35 UTC

**Decided and built**

1. **After noon, the card stops asking.** A session's answer is the first
   check-in of the morning band on its day and the next; after noon the
   card kept its Check in button, the check-in landed, the card did not
   move, and it asked again. `dailyGuidance` now takes the minute of the
   day; outside the band it says the session is in and how a session is
   read — never that a morning was missed. The band's edges are named in
   `metrics.ts`. Said out loud: this is a clock-driven change within one
   day, a small exception to "between two opens nothing changes"; the
   only thing noon removes is an ask that could not be met.
2. **Onboarding opens on "Understand your pain."** The landing's first
   sentence, not its second: "Keep living" is an order to someone who may
   be mid-flare. Apple Health is named conditionally — it is not asked
   for until Today, so a watch sentence would be false for everyone on
   day one.
3. **The clinician report lost three verdicts.** "As a record improves,
   the milder rows grow", "Lower is better on this chart", and "your
   entries" in a page about "the patient". Rejected: replacing "(may be
   worth watching)" with "met Pattern's reporting bar" — jargon to a
   clinician, and it would drop the dose note's content.
4. **The report says how the chosen activity has been going.** One line
   under "Chosen activity": sessions since a date with check-ins both
   mornings, how many went fine — defined inline with
   `CAPACITY_HARDER_POINTS`, since paper has no card note — and the
   median length of the sessions that went fine, earliest window against
   latest. The latest window is exactly the card's level, so page and
   card cannot disagree. No pain value, no verdict word, silent below the
   card's gate. About two and a half lines: dropping "with check-ins both
   mornings" would make the count read as all sessions.
5. **`week_active` — on its own PR (#22), for the owner to decide.** It
   changes what leaves the phone and edits the privacy policy, so it is
   not the team's call to merge. A week number 1–13, nothing else; read
   by cohort against `first_checkin`, no install id.

**Process note:** two agents wrote patch scripts to the same scratch path;
one was applied by mistake, caught at typecheck, reverted from git.
Patch files now go in per-agent folders.

## Round 4 · 21:31–21:42 UTC

**Briefs:** founder bug-hunts rounds 2–3; design audits every colour
against "colour means pain"; copy finds the last things with two names;
GTM walks a new watch-wearing runner through days 0–7 in the code;
research checks the evidence behind leaning on mornings.

**Decided and built**

1. **Session before the morning check-in (founder, medium).** A run at
   seven, the app at nine: the card said "Tomorrow morning's check-in
   will show how it went" with no button — false, since this morning's
   check-in is needed too, and the session would quietly count for
   nothing. It now shows the button and says both mornings count.
2. **Colour means pain, enforced.** `color.tint` (#5BA8FF, between ramp
   stops 3 and 4) is white, so links stop wearing a score; switches take
   the neutral grey so the white thumb stays visible. Onboarding chips
   and the diagnosis selection used `themeBrand()` — ramp stop 5 — and
   now use the white treatment the activity card already had. The
   calendar's today number is white. Onboarding's example week drew one
   hue at varying opacity, which read backwards since the ramp flipped.
3. **One name for each thing.** "A few seconds" (was ten, a few, and
   twenty). The check-in asks "How intense is your pain right now?" like
   the eleven notifications; an edit asks "How intense was your pain?".
   The app's own file is "a saved copy" everywhere — Profile said
   "Export backup", and "backup" also names the iPhone's, which does not
   restore a reinstall, so a tester following the TestFlight note could
   not find the row. Ability "runs the other way", not "better".
4. **The note says a morning can be harder for other reasons.** For
   inflammatory pain the morning follows the body clock and a flare can
   raise it two points with no help from yesterday's walk. Comparing
   morning with morning removes the time of day, not disease swings. The
   card's own note now says so, and why one harder morning only holds.
   Sources: Silbernagel 2007; Cutolo & Straub, ARD 2008; EULAR 2018.
5. **The empty card says where sessions come from.** GTM's walkthrough:
   a watch runner with months of runs was told "No sessions recorded
   yet", with no way forward, until Health was connected — and Today's
   Health offer comes after the reminder and can be dismissed for good.
   While Health has never been asked for, the card now says Pattern
   can't see sessions yet, that they come from Apple Health, and that it
   is in Profile. Text only; no button, layout untouched.

**Corrected on the record:** round 1's claim that the 90-day Health
backfill gives a watch wearer an answer on day one is false — every
backfilled session has no morning check-ins around it. The first real
answer comes about day 6 at three runs a week, whenever Health is
connected. So Health-first ordering stays parked: it gains nothing.

**Built**

   the last button, so Back on every step after the first is safe: each

   44pt, VoiceOver escape gesture does the same. Screens sit 20pt lower.
2. **Tab labels scale with Dynamic Type** up to 1.2×, the most the

3. **Fresh-eyes copy.** "Next time, 44 minutes" — no "go for"; the

## Round 6 · 21:52–22:00 UTC

**Briefs:** research audits Patterns against the refusals; copy reads the
day and logging sheets as someone mid-flare with one hand; design audits
their accessibility; GTM writes the competitor brief; the founder reviews
round 5 and lists what in AGENTS.md today made stale.

**Built**

1. **Patterns stopped grading the halves (violation).** It showed "1.2
   lower — easier" between the two halves of a window — a delta on pain
   with a verdict word, which one check-in could flip between two opens
   of the same day. Now both averages, as the PDF always printed, with
   "Averages of what you entered, not a measure of how you are doing."
   `DIRECTION_SAME_BELOW` retired. The stricter reading — drop the halves
   from Patterns entirely — is one line, and the owner's call.
2. **Every stand-out card says what it is not, inside it** (violation):
   each card's caveat or disclaimer was only in its sheet or behind the
   section heading's (i). The section note no longer claims only a
   check-in moves it — Apple Health adding a day does too.
3. **Day and logging copy.** A failed save said "Nothing was recorded"
   to someone whose words were still on screen; it now says so. A flare
   logged during it is asked "How long has it lasted?". "No check-ins on
   this day" when an event shows below. "The PDF" as one name. A skip is
   no longer "a fine answer".
4. **Day screens for tremor and VoiceOver.** Edit / Add information /
   Delete and calendar days reach 44pt by hitSlop; VoiceOver steps days
   by swiping up or down on the date (a sideways swipe moves focus, not
   the pager); the Today jump respects Reduce Motion; "out of 10" and
   "3 to 6" are spoken, not "slash" and "dash"; flare questions are
   headings, and a chosen pill says tapping again clears it.
5. **Founder lows:** a shorter header on onboarding's first screen (SE
   fold); the Log tab's large-content title says "Log"; the fold's
   waiting line is true after noon.
6. `docs/COMPETITORS.md` — nearest rival is Exakt (EU medical device for
   runners, next-day pain after rehab sessions, its own plan). The claim
   never to make: that Pattern says how much activity is safe.
