You have been let down
by trackers before.

The streak that shamed you on a flare day. The “insight” that was a guess. The export button that went to a server. Pattern is built by someone who has the pain and hated all of it. So it promises:

  • No streaks. No guilt.A flare is not a failure. Nothing counts your missed days.
  • No made-up scores.The number is the one you gave. Nobody averages your pain into a “wellness index.”
  • No guessing at causes.Nothing is called a trigger until your own days have shown it, with the numbers on the card.
  • No “down 12% this week.”Nothing moves unless you add to it. You will never open the app to be judged.
  • No medication advice. Ever.That is between you and your doctor.
  • No account. No upload.Nothing about your body leaves your phone. There is no server to leak it.
  • No colour for decoration.Every colour on screen is a pain level. Nothing else is allowed to look like one.
  • No AI deciding what you feel.Plain arithmetic, with the thresholds written down.
