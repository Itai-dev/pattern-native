02 · Your life fills in the rest

You live your life.
Pattern connects the dots.

Your watch already knows how you slept. Your phone knows how far you walked and how long the class ran. Pattern reads them from Apple Health, on the phone, and lays them beside the number you gave. Nothing else to log.

03 · It says what it can

Patterns, in plain words.
Or an honest “not yet”.

When enough of your own days point the same way, Pattern says so, with the numbers behind it. Until then it says how many days it still needs. It never fills the silence with a guess.

04 · Before the decision

The sentence before
the workout.

Once your record has a line in it, Pattern speaks before you cross it: before the session, the evening before the booking, the fortnight you decide to test something. The part no other tracker does.

Tomorrow morning is decided
this evening.

A record that only looks back tells you what you already know by the time you read it. Pattern reads your own record forward and puts the sentence where the decision is.

Your usual workout, and the minutes past which your next mornings ran harder — shown before you train. The class tomorrow that runs past that line, shown tonight, with one tap into your calendar. And one thing you want to test, for a fortnight, with an honest answer at the end.

Every sentence describes your own record. Never “stop”, never “you should”, never a dose. Exercise helps most persistent pain; the target is the session that costs tomorrow, not moving.

Sleep, steps and workouts arrive from Apple Health on their own, beside the number you gave. Garmin, Oura, WHOOP, Strava — anything that writes to Health — arrive the same way. Nothing you tell it about your body
leaves the phone.

No account. No sign-in. No server that has ever held a pain score. Your record is one file inside the app, protected by your passcode, and it travels only in your iPhone’s own backup or in a summary you choose to share.

  • Local, by design.The record is SQLite on the phone. Deleting the app deletes it. Pattern cannot see it, sell it or lose it for you.
  • Counts, never content.Anonymous usage counts — that a check-in happened, never what it said — go to an EU-hosted service you can switch off in Profile.
  • Your words stay yours.Notes, your background, what you want to ask the doctor: printed exactly as written, read by nothing, sent nowhere.

Weeks of your actual days on one page, in the order a clinician asks. Made on your phone. Sent only where you send it. Better information for your doctor — the diagnosis, if one comes, is theirs.

You have been let down
by trackers before.

The streak that shamed you on a flare day. The insight that was a guess. Pattern is built by someone who has the pain and still wanted to run. Here is what you can count on.

  • A missed day is just a missed day.A flare is not a failure, and nothing keeps score.
  • The number is yours.Exactly as you gave it. Never averaged into a score you did not enter.
  • Pattern waits for evidence.An association appears only once enough of your own days support it, with the numbers on the card, and it tells you when it cannot say yet.
  • Never a diagnosis, never a dose.It records what a clinician has told you. It does not suggest one, and medication stays between you and your doctor.
  • Nothing rewards looking at your pain.No rings, no deltas, no verdict on today. What moves is a countdown to an answer, and only when you add a day.
  • Your record stays on your phone.No Pattern account, and no server that holds health data.

For the ones who read the spec.

StorageLocal SQLite. No account. Deleting the app deletes the record; a backup you keep in Files does not.
AnswersThree states, kept apart: never asked, skipped, answered. A skip is never read as a no.
ThresholdsNamed, in one file, with the arithmetic that argues for each. Open to argument.
AssociationsDeterministic, on device. Your own lowest and highest third, middle third set aside. A card appears only past the gates — eighteen paired days, five per group, 1.5 points of pain, the same direction in both halves of the record. Gates, not proof.
Before the decisionThe workout line is the first load of your own harder tercile, from the same association, shown only once that association clears every gate. The calendar is read on the phone and never stored; Pattern never writes to it.
ColourEvery colour on screen is a pain level. Nothing else is allowed to look like one.
HealthRead-only. Per-category consent through Apple’s own sheet. Imported values are never written into Pattern’s backup file, and live in a folder iOS leaves out of backups.
WatchSends the number only. The phone is the only writer.
AnalyticsAnonymous counts that something happened, never what it said. Aptabase, EU-hosted. Off in Profile.
