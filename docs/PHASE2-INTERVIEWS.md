# Phase 2 — talking to ten people

*Written 18 Oct 2026, after the team loop (`TEAM-LOOP-2026-10-18.md`).
ROADMAP owns the kill criterion — fewer than 5/10 logging at week 4 — and
this file owns how the founder finds out, and what he may write down
while he does. Where the two disagree, ROADMAP wins.*

The analytics cannot answer the Phase 2 question — no install id, so a
week-4 open cannot be tied to a tester (GROWTH §2). The ten conversations
are the numbers, so the protocol is fixed before the first call: answers
asked ten different ways cannot be added.

## 0. Three rules for every contact

1. **Behaviour, in the past, specifically.** "Walk me through the last
   time" — never "would you", "do you like", "is it useful". People are
   kind to founders, and opinions about the future cost nothing to give
   (Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*). The one exception is a comprehension
   check ("in your words, what does X mean?"): it tests the copy.
2. **The founder is a reminder too.** Every email and call prompts a
   check-in, so contact is fixed by calendar, identical for all ten, and
   never reacts to how much someone has logged — which he cannot see
   anyway. Nothing he sends mentions a missed day, a count of days, "keep
   it up" or "haven't heard from you". AGENTS.md's "never penalise a
   missed one" binds his inbox exactly as it binds the app.
3. **Notes hold the app, never the body.** If it would be a row in their
   record, it does not go in the notes (§5).

## 1. Screening — the invite email

The landing page's email opens with three optional questions. Each
answers one fit question, and none is a gate on its own:

| Question in the email | What it is actually for |
|---|---|
| What are you trying to get back to, or keep doing? | The activity card opens on that goal. "To be pain-free" or "nothing, really" means the card has nothing to stand on — a conversation, not a no. |
| Apple Watch, or anything that writes to Health? | Sessions reach the card only through Health. Without them it stays at "still learning" and we test the diary, not the product. |
| What kind of pain? In your own words. | Phase 2's ICP is recurring musculoskeletal or inflammatory pain in someone active (POSITIONING, "Living actively with pain"). Also where a red flag may surface early (§1.2). |

**A blank reply is not a no.** The email says "answer any, or none"; an
unanswered question is skipped, not negative — the app's three-state rule
applies to the founder too. Invite, and ask on day 0.

### 1.1 Who to say no to, or not yet

- **No iPhone.** iOS only, no Android planned. A clean no.
- **Pain not tied to activity** — migraine, pelvic pain, widespread pain
  with no movement goal. The diary would work; the card, which is what
  Phase 2 tests, would not. Not yet, and say why.
- **A new injury or surgery in the last six weeks, or a rehab protocol
  with set progressions.** Their clinician's plan governs, and a card
  offering "a little longer" would compete with it. Not yet.
- **Looking for a diagnosis or a treatment.** Pattern makes neither; they
  leave at week 2 for an honest reason that teaches us nothing.
- **In crisis** — the email reads as pain having taken over everything
  right now. A person who needs more than an app: reply warmly, §1.2.
- **No watch or Health:** a maybe. At most two, marked.
- **Knows the founder personally:** at most two, marked. Friends stay out
  of kindness; if the pass depends on them, it did not pass.

**The no, kindly.** Specific about the app's limit, never about them, and
it leaves a door. Then delete their reply — a no keeps nothing:

> Thank you for writing — it helped. Right now Pattern is built around
> [one line: iPhone only / an activity you're building back to / not
> during a new injury], so this round wouldn't be fair to your time. Can
> I write once when that changes? I won't keep what you told me.

### 1.2 The safety line

Pattern is not a clinical service and the founder is not a clinician. He
does not assess, advise, reassure or triage. If, in any email or call, a
tester says something that sounds like thoughts of suicide or self-harm,
or describes as **new**: loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness
between the legs, legs getting weaker, back pain with fever or
unexplained weight loss, or pain after a fall or crash —

- Stop the agenda. Say one plain thing: *"That sounds like something to
  get seen about today. Please call your doctor, or [emergency number]."*
  For suicidal thoughts, name a crisis line: Israel ERAN 1201 (emergency
  101), UK Samaritans 116 123 (999), US 988 (911), EU 112, elsewhere
  findahelpline.com. Ask on day 0 which country they are in.
- Never interpret, diagnose, guess, or say it is probably fine. Do not
  mention the app or their logging. Offer to end the call.
- Note **only** "signposted, date". Whether they stay a tester is their
  call, asked once, a week later.

## 2. Day 0 — the onboarding call (15 min)

The TestFlight invite goes out on the wave's Monday (PHASE2-LAUNCH §3):
install, do not open. Calls are Tuesday to Thursday of that week, so the
first check-in lands inside the cohort week — and the day of that first
check-in is **day 0**, the anchor of every day number below. Call by
voice or video **without screen-share** — a shared screen shows the first
pain number, and a recording keeps it. Think-aloud instead.

**Script (2 min, read it):** "Thanks for doing this. I'm testing the app,
not you — anything confusing is my fault and exactly what I need. It isn't
medical advice and it isn't a study. I'll write down what you do in the
app and what you say about it; I won't write down anything about your
pain, and you never need to tell me a number. You can stop any time, and
stopping tells me as much as staying. Please open it now and say out loud
what you're reading and thinking. I'll mostly be quiet."

**Then say nothing for about ten minutes.** Stuck past ~30 seconds: "What
are you looking for?" — never the answer. Watch, do not ask:

- A first check-in unaided, and roughly how long? (GROWTH §5: the
  highest-leverage number in the funnel.)
- What they read aloud, what they skip, where the voice slows.
- The goal screen: picked, own words, or skipped — write which.
- Health and the reminder, when Today offers them: taken? which slot?
  What do they say while deciding?
- Why they want this, verbatim — if it is about the app, not the body.

**Last 3 minutes, the same words to all ten:** mornings matter most,
because the card reads each session against the next morning; before
deleting or reinstalling, save a copy in Profile; reply to this email for
anything, and I'll call in about ten days.

## 3. Week 1 — the check-in (10 min, day 8–10)

Not day 7: the card's first answer needs three sessions, each with a
check-in that morning and the next — about day 6 at three runs a week
(TEAM-LOOP round 4). This call is the week-1 contact; there is no
separate day-8 email. Six questions, in order; the follow-ups are the only
permitted prompts.

1. **"Walk me through the last time you opened Pattern — where were
   you, what was going on?"** Then: "And the time before?" (Is there a
   moment it lives in, or is it random?)
2. **"Tell me about a usual morning, from waking to leaving the house.
   Where did Pattern land in that, if it did?"** Then: "Did a
   notification come? What did you do with it?" Tests round 1's morning
   reminder, placed half an hour after usual wake. Write *fits / too
   early / too late / ignored / evening slot / none*.
3. **"The activity card says a session 'went fine'. In your words, what
   does Pattern mean by that?"** Right: the next morning was not two or
   more points harder than this one. Listen for "it didn't hurt during",
   "I felt good after", "no pain". Write *right / partly / wrong* and
   the quote.
4. **"Has Today ever said nothing longer was suggested? What did you
   make of it?"** — not "did you have a flare". Tests round 2's flare-day line
   without asking about the flare. If no, move on; never explain it.
5. **"Has the card told you anything other than 'still learning' yet?
   What did you do the next time you went out?"** Write *not yet /
   arrived*, then *followed / did more / did less / didn't notice*.
   Never the minutes — session lengths are their record.
6. **"What's the last thing in the app that made you stop and go
   'huh'?"** Then: "Is there anything you've stopped doing in it?"

Never ask whether they like it, and never correct a wrong read — it is
the finding, and correcting it contaminates week 4.

## 4. Week 4 — the exit conversation (from the wave's read Monday)

Week 4 is days 21–27. The email goes on the Monday after the wave's
week-4 window closes — 30 Nov, 14 Dec, 28 Dec (PHASE2-LAUNCH §3) — which
is day 29–35 for anyone whose day 0 fell in the cohort week, so the call
cannot be the reason they logged that week. Calls happen that week.
**One email, identical for all ten**: the founder does not know who
stopped and must not sound as if he does.

> It's been four weeks — thank you. Whatever happened with the app is
> useful to me, including if it dropped out of your days early on; that's
> the result I most need to understand. Ten minutes on a call, or two
> lines by reply, whichever is easier.

**First, the count.** "Open History and count the days from [date of
day 21] to [date of day 27] with any check-in — just the number of
days." Read off their
screen, said by them, nothing else: recalled logging runs far above real
logging (Stone 2002, below). It goes first, before the founder can lead.

**If still logging:**

1. "Walk me through the last week — when did you open it, and what for?"
2. "What did the card tell you most recently? What did you do after?"
3. "Has anything from Pattern come up with someone else — a physio, a
   doctor, a training partner?" (GROWTH step 4's gate starts here.)
4. "Was there anything you went looking for and didn't find?" — how to
   hear GROWTH §6's "pain down 12% this week" without suggesting it.
5. "What would make you stop?"

**If stopped — the one question that matters:**

> **"What was going on the week Pattern stopped fitting?"**

The app is the subject of that sentence, not the person. Not "why did
you stop", not "what would have kept you" (an invitation to design the
product for us, kindly). Listen. One follow-up only: "What's the last
thing you remember it showing you?" — it locates the rung of GROWTH §1's
ladder they fell from. If the answer is a hard stretch with their pain,
say thank you and move on, and code it *bad-week fit*: whether the app
holds someone through a bad week is the finding; the week is not.

End both the same: keep it, delete it, or have their notes deleted —
none of these is a disappointment.

## 5. What to record, and what never to

AGENTS.md: no plaintext of the health record exists anywhere but the
phone, and no support or debugging path may create one. Interview notes
are a support path.

**Notes may hold:** a pseudonym (T01–T15), channel, watch y/n, what they
did in the app, the §3 codes, verbatim quotes **about the app**,
confusion points, contact dates, the week-4 day count, a reason code.

**Notes never hold:** a pain number, diagnosis, body area, medication,
treatment, flare, symptom, session length, a card sentence with their
minutes in it, or a quote containing any of these — cut the app-part out
or drop it. No call recordings, no transcripts, no screenshots.

**Week-4 reason codes:** *friction* (too long, too many steps) ·
*confusion* (didn't understand what it said) · *no answer* (the card
never said anything worth opening for) · *notifications* (wrong time,
too many, none) · *bad-week fit* (the app didn't serve a hard stretch —
from the app's side, no detail) · *life* (travel, busy, phone) · *not
said*.

**Where they live:** one spreadsheet on the founder's own device. Not in
this repo, an issue or a commit, not pasted into an AI session, not in
Aptabase. PHASE2-LAUNCH's cohort ledger is this sheet, not a second
one. The name ↔ T-code map lives only in his email contacts.

**The screening replies are the known exception.** Question 3 puts a
description of someone's pain in a Gmail inbox. Volunteered, and not the
record, but the most sensitive thing Phase 2 creates: decide, note only
the fit, delete every reply when Phase 2 closes. If two months feels too
long to hold it, the fix is dropping question 3, not copying it somewhere
safer.

**Logging at week 4** means a check-in on **at least two separate days
in days 21–27** — the same seven days `week_active` calls week 4 (days
0–6 are week 1, `src/weeks.ts` on PR #22). Not one: a single day can be
a stray notification or one curious open, and two is a return. Not three
or more: someone who logs only on hard days is using it as built, and a
missed day is just a missed day. This is Phase 2's number, in this file
and in PHASE2-LAUNCH alike.

**How it relates to `week_active{week:4}`.** That event says a phone
made a check-in on *at least one* day of the same window, and nothing
from anyone who turned analytics off. So per wave it is a cross-check,
never the count: it should be at least the number of two-day loggers
who left analytics on, and if it is lower, a date or a count is wrong —
ask again, do not adjust. A gap above that is one-day weeks. It cannot
turn a no-reply into a yes.

**The denominator is everyone who had a day-0 call.** Nobody leaves it
for a good reason — a lost phone, a busy month, a bad week are what real
use looks like. Never installed: reported, not counted. **No reply at
week 4 is *unknown*, never "stopped"** — three states — but the pass
needs five known (eight of fifteen), so an unknown cannot count toward it.

**The rule, written now so it cannot be argued later:**

- **≥5 of 10 logging** (at least half, rounded up: 8 of 15 if wave 3
  tops up, GROWTH Step 3) — Phase 2 passes, unless the pass needs a
  tester who knows the founder; then it does not.
- **<5 of 10** — the kill criterion has fired. Read the reason codes,
  not the quotes. Mostly *friction / confusion / notifications*: fix the
  named thing, then a **fresh** ten, never the same ten again. Mostly
  *no answer*: the activity card's thesis is wrong, which is a
  POSITIONING question before it is a build.
- **Whatever the count:** three or more *wrong* on "went fine" means the
  copy is rewritten before Phase 3. Half or more at *not yet* by week 1
  means round 1's worry — the card learns only from mornings — was
  right, and that is the first fix.

**Between calls, Today does not change** (the team loop's standing
rule): a week-4 number means nothing if the screen moved under it. Fix
crashes and anything that blocks a check-in; queue the rest, and date
every change that ships so the table can be read against it.

**Two dates.** Build 48 expires about 14 Dec, inside waves 2 and 3's
week 4 — an expiry in week 4 is a stopped tester the app did not cause.
So every day 0 is on build 49 (PHASE2-LAUNCH §2), which stops about 19
Jan, after the last week-4 call; if 49 slips, every wave moves two weeks
with it. PR #22 (`week_active`), if merged, cross-checks the day counts;
it never replaces asking.
